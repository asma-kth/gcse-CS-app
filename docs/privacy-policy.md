# Privacy Policy for Byte Quest

**Last updated:** 9 September 2026

Byte Quest is a revision app for the OCR GCSE Computer Science specification. This policy explains,
in plain English, exactly what the app does with information. The short version is that it does
not collect anything.

## What we collect

Nothing. Byte Quest has no accounts, no sign up, no login and no analytics. We do not ask for your
name, your email address, your age, your school, or any other personal detail, and there is no way
for us to receive any of it.

## What is stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your own phone or tablet, using the standard storage that Android
provides to the app. That record contains only:

- how much XP and how many data shards you have earned
- which lessons, quizzes and papers you have completed, and your best scores
- your daily streak and which fun facts you have already seen

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, or use the reset button
on the progress screen, it is deleted.

## Network use

Byte Quest works entirely offline. Once installed it does not send or receive any data, so nothing
about your use of it travels anywhere.

## Advertising

There is no advertising in Byte Quest, and no advertising identifier is collected.

## Children

Byte Quest is intended for students studying GCSE Computer Science, typically aged 14 to 16. Since
the app collects no personal data at all, there is nothing to disclose, sell or share about any
user, of any age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Third parties

The app contains no third party analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind. Nothing is
shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

## Permissions

The app requests only the standard internet permission that Android requires of an app built this
way. It is not used to transmit anything about you.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to correct, export or erase on request.
The data on your device is under your control and you can clear it at any time using the reset
button on the progress screen, or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with information, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
